The source/ directory contains the exact upstream source archives, dependency
source archives (including their original notices and build recipes), and
EffeTune's build instructions and build-only adaptations. The source filenames,
revisions, versions, license metadata, and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in
runtime-manifest.json and source/effetune-airplay-build/native/airplay-receiver/
dependencies.lock.json. These materials may be used to rebuild, modify, and
replace the receiver and its libraries. EffeTune does not restrict replacement
of the receiver. UxPlay is a separate executable; its GNU GPL licensing and
the licenses of every redistributed dependency continue to apply.

The fixed UxPlay build promotes its existing connection-count diagnostics to
normal log level and flushes log output. This exposes actual connection
teardown without guessing from audio silence. Its existing volume callback
also emits the computed sender slider fraction as a flushed framed record;
the volume calculation and application are unchanged. It does not change its AirPlay,
authentication, cryptography, discovery, or media protocol implementation.
The build recipe contains the complete, checked adaptation.

UxPlay's upstream README contains a FairPlay-related disclaimer concerning
third-party code and the legal uncertainty identified by its authors. That
README and all source notices are included unchanged in the source archive.
This distribution makes no claim of Apple certification, DRM playback,
AirPlay 2 multiroom support, or compatibility with future Apple software.

The Windows build restores the missing LOG_ERR Windows debug-level mapping,
removes obsolete references to the already-removed legacy NAT implementation,
and compiles source as UTF-8. The source-built service is installed only by
the normal elevated installer when no Bonjour Service already exists.
Portable builds require an existing compatible discovery service. EffeTune
does not replace or uninstall a preexisting shared discovery service.

GStreamer, FFmpeg, GLib, OpenSSL, libplist and runtime dependencies
---------------------------------------------------------------
Exact redistributed packages and licenses are listed in runtime-manifest.json.
Their original license files are included under licenses/ and their complete
corresponding source archives under source/. GStreamer components are generally
LGPL-2.1-or-later; UxPlay's complete combined binary retains its GNU GPL terms.
FFmpeg is built from the fixed upstream source with AAC/ALAC codecs and no
external codec libraries. Unneeded GStreamer video sinks are not included.
The upstream-required parser/autodetection plugins remain available because
the fixed UxPlay version checks their presence even in audio-only mode.

Windows dependency binaries are obtained from the official MSYS2 UCRT64
repository, verified against locked SHA-256 checksums, and selected by their
actual PE imports. FFmpeg is rebuilt with the small audio-only configuration
in the build recipe rather than using the general-purpose vendor FFmpeg
binary and its unrelated video and speech-recognition dependencies.

Linux uses the system's existing Avahi/DNS-SD service. macOS uses the operating
system's Bonjour service. EffeTune does not start or stop those system services.
