## Short description

Shape music playback with real-time audio effects, equalization, and visualizations.

## Description

EffeTune is an audio player and real-time effects processor for people who enjoy listening closely to music. Adjust the sound while music plays, combine effects in a drag-and-drop pipeline, and save your settings as presets.

Play local music files, browse selected folders in Music Library, or use the desktop Browser to listen to supported websites through your effects. Equalizers adjust the balance of frequency ranges, dynamics effects control changing levels, and visualizers help you inspect the signal.

Start with a local music file and a simple equalizer. Add or bypass effects to compare the result, then save a preset when you find settings you want to use again. You can also process selected audio files and save the results.

Optional local-network features let compatible OpenHome controllers manage playback or let a paired browser control effects. These features can be turned off when you do not need them.

Playback from third-party websites depends on the website and its sign-in, playback, and content-protection requirements. EffeTune does not provide a music subscription or guarantee compatibility with every streaming service. Processing audio from a separate desktop application may require additional audio routing software.

## Features

- Real-time effect chains with drag-and-drop ordering and bypass comparison
- Equalization, dynamics processing, and other audio effects
- Local music playback, folder browsing, metadata, and playlists
- Desktop Browser audio processing for supported websites
- Presets, live visualizers, and offline audio-file processing
- Optional OpenHome playback control and paired remote effect control
